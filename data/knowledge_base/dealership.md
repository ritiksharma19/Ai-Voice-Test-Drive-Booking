# Aurora Motors dealership information

## Showrooms and timings

### Showroom locations
Aurora Motors has two showrooms in Pune. Baner Showroom is on Baner Road near Balewadi Phata. Viman Nagar Showroom is on Nagar Road opposite Phoenix Marketcity. Both showrooms have all four models on display and free customer parking.

### Opening hours
Both showrooms are open every day of the week, including Sundays, from 10 AM to 7 PM. Test drives and sales meetings can be booked for any hour between 10 AM and 6 PM.

## Test drives

### Test drive policy
Test drives are free. The customer must be at least 18 years old and carry a valid original driving licence. A test drive lasts about 20 to 30 minutes on a fixed route with a sales consultant in the car. Every model can be test driven at both showrooms.

### Home test drive
Home test drives are available for the Aurora Ridge and Aurora Ion within 15 km of either showroom, subject to availability. Customers can ask for a home test drive while booking.

### Test drive booking and cancellation
Test drives can be booked up to 30 days in advance, in one-hour slots between 10 AM and 6 PM. To reschedule or cancel, the customer can call the showroom or ask the voice assistant, quoting the booking ID.

## Sales meetings

### Sales consultation meeting
A sales meeting is a one-to-one session with a sales consultant at the showroom to discuss variants, on-road price, finance, exchange of an old car and delivery dates. It takes about 45 minutes. Meetings can be booked in one-hour slots between 10 AM and 6 PM.

## Buying process

### Booking amount and delivery
A car can be booked with a fully refundable booking amount of 21,000 rupees. Typical delivery is 2 to 4 weeks for the Pico and Sprint, and 4 to 8 weeks for the Ridge and Ion, depending on variant and colour.

### Documents needed to buy a car
Proof of identity such as Aadhaar or passport, proof of address, PAN card, and passport size photographs. For a car loan, the bank also needs income proof such as salary slips or income tax returns.
