# Aurora Motors finance, warranty and service

## Finance and exchange

### Car loan and EMI
Aurora Motors works with partner banks and finance companies for car loans of up to 90 percent of the on-road price and tenures of up to 7 years. Interest rates depend on the lender and the customer's credit profile, so the exact rate and EMI are confirmed by the sales consultant.

### Exchange of old car
Customers can exchange any old car of any brand. The car is evaluated free at the showroom or at home, and the exchange value is adjusted against the new car price. An additional exchange bonus may apply during offers.

### Offers and discounts
Current offers change every month and differ by model and variant. The sales consultant confirms the latest offers during a sales meeting.

## Warranty and service

### Standard warranty
Every Aurora car comes with a standard warranty of 3 years or 1 lakh km, whichever is earlier. Extended warranty up to 5 years or 1.5 lakh km can be bought at the time of purchase. The Aurora Ion battery and motor are covered for 8 years or 1.6 lakh km.

### Service schedule and cost
Periodic service is due every 10,000 km or 12 months, whichever is earlier. The first three services are free of labour charges. Both Pune showrooms have service workshops with pick-up and drop.

### Roadside assistance
24 by 7 roadside assistance is free for the first 3 years, including towing, flat tyre help and battery jump-start.
