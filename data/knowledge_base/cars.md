# Aurora Motors car lineup

## Aurora Pico (compact hatchback, ऑरोरा पिको)

### Aurora Pico overview
The Aurora Pico is a five-seat compact hatchback for city driving and first-time buyers. It is easy to park, light to steer and economical to run.

### Aurora Pico price and variants
Ex-showroom prices in Pune: Pico E manual 5.99 lakh rupees, Pico S manual 6.79 lakh rupees, Pico SX AMT automatic 7.89 lakh rupees. On-road price adds registration, insurance and road tax, roughly 10 to 12 percent more.

### Aurora Pico engine, mileage and gearbox
1.2 litre three-cylinder petrol engine, 83 PS and 113 Nm. Five-speed manual or five-speed AMT automatic. ARAI mileage 20.5 km per litre manual and 21.1 km per litre AMT. Fuel tank 37 litres.

### Aurora Pico features and safety
Six airbags standard on all variants, ABS with EBD, ESC, rear parking camera from S variant, 7 inch touchscreen with Android Auto and Apple CarPlay, automatic climate control on SX. Boot space 300 litres. Ground clearance 170 mm.

### Aurora Pico colours
Arctic White, Graphite Grey, Fiery Red and Ocean Blue.

## Aurora Sprint (mid-size sedan, ऑरोरा स्प्रिंट)

### Aurora Sprint overview
The Aurora Sprint is a five-seat mid-size sedan with a large boot and a comfortable ride for families and highway driving.

### Aurora Sprint price and variants
Ex-showroom prices in Pune: Sprint S manual 10.49 lakh rupees, Sprint SX manual 12.19 lakh rupees, Sprint SX CVT automatic 13.39 lakh rupees, Sprint ZX CVT automatic 14.29 lakh rupees.

### Aurora Sprint engine, mileage and gearbox
1.5 litre four-cylinder petrol engine, 115 PS and 144 Nm. Six-speed manual or CVT automatic. ARAI mileage 18.2 km per litre manual and 19.0 km per litre CVT. Fuel tank 45 litres.

### Aurora Sprint features and safety
Six airbags, ESC and hill-hold standard. Electric sunroof, ventilated front seats and wireless charging from SX. Level 1 ADAS driver assistance on ZX, including forward collision warning and lane keep assist. 10.25 inch touchscreen. Boot space 480 litres, the largest in its class.

### Aurora Sprint colours
Pearl White, Titanium Silver, Midnight Black and Burgundy Red.

## Aurora Ridge (7-seater SUV, ऑरोरा रिज)

### Aurora Ridge overview
The Aurora Ridge is a seven-seat SUV for large families, long trips and rough roads, with an optional all-wheel drive.

### Aurora Ridge price and variants
Ex-showroom prices in Pune: Ridge LX diesel manual 16.99 lakh rupees, Ridge VX diesel automatic 20.49 lakh rupees, Ridge ZX diesel automatic 22.99 lakh rupees, Ridge ZX AWD diesel automatic 24.49 lakh rupees.

### Aurora Ridge engine, mileage and gearbox
2.0 litre turbo diesel engine, 170 PS and 380 Nm. Six-speed manual or six-speed torque converter automatic. All-wheel drive on ZX AWD. ARAI mileage 15.1 km per litre manual and 14.3 km per litre automatic. Fuel tank 60 litres.

### Aurora Ridge features and safety
Seven airbags, level 2 ADAS with adaptive cruise control on ZX, 360 degree camera, panoramic sunroof, three-row air conditioning vents, captain seats option in the second row. Ground clearance 210 mm. Boot space 180 litres with all seats up and 710 litres with the third row folded.

### Aurora Ridge colours
Glacier White, Forest Green, Slate Grey and Desert Bronze.

## Aurora Ion (electric SUV, ऑरोरा आयन)

### Aurora Ion overview
The Aurora Ion is a five-seat compact electric SUV with zero tailpipe emissions and very low running cost, about one rupee per kilometre when charged at home.

### Aurora Ion price and variants
Ex-showroom prices in Pune: Ion Standard Range 17.49 lakh rupees, Ion Long Range 19.99 lakh rupees, Ion Long Range Plus 21.99 lakh rupees. A 7.2 kW home wall charger and its installation are included free.

### Aurora Ion battery, range and charging
Standard Range: 38 kWh battery, 360 km certified range. Long Range: 45 kWh battery, 465 km certified range. Real-world city range is typically 75 to 85 percent of the certified figure. DC fast charging at 60 kW takes 10 to 80 percent in about 40 minutes. The 7.2 kW AC home charger fills the battery from 10 to 100 percent in about 6.5 hours.

### Aurora Ion motor and performance
Single front motor, 150 PS and 250 Nm. 0 to 100 km per hour in 8.9 seconds. Single-speed automatic, three regenerative braking levels and one-pedal driving.

### Aurora Ion battery warranty and features
Battery and motor warranty of 8 years or 1.6 lakh km, whichever is earlier. Six airbags, level 2 ADAS on Long Range Plus, connected car app with remote climate control, vehicle-to-load power outlet, panoramic glass roof. Boot space 420 litres plus a 25 litre front trunk.

### Aurora Ion colours
Polar White, Electric Teal, Space Grey and Sunset Orange.

## Choosing a model

### Which Aurora car suits me
City commute on a budget: Aurora Pico. Family sedan with a big boot and highway comfort: Aurora Sprint. Seven seats, long trips or rough roads: Aurora Ridge. Lowest running cost, home charging available, mostly city and short highway trips: Aurora Ion electric.
